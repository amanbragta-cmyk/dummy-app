# User journeys

Each screen is walked and judged against these flows before its task closes.

| id | who | their job | where it starts | steps | what shows when it is done |
|----|-----|-----------|-----------------|-------|----------------------------|
| J01 | home cook | find a recipe using an ingredient I have | /#/ | type "chickpea" in search; open the first result | the recipe page shows its photo, ingredients with quantities and numbered steps |
| J02 | home cook | narrow recipes to what suits tonight | /#/ | tap the "vegetarian" tag; tap the "under 30 min" tag | only recipes with both tags are listed; clearing the tags shows all recipes again |
| J03 | home cook | keep a recipe I like | /#/ | open a recipe; tap the heart; open Favourites; reload the page | the recipe is listed in Favourites and is still there after the reload |
| J04 | home cook | shop for this week's meals | /#/ | open a recipe; tap "Add to shopping list"; open a second recipe; tap "Add to shopping list"; open Shopping list | ingredients from both recipes are listed, with the same ingredient and unit combined into one line |
| J05 | home cook | tick off what I have bought | /#/list | tick two items; tap "Clear ticked" | only the unticked items remain, and that is still true after a reload |
